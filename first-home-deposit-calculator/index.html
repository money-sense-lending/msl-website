Assumptions this uses
  • Saving is linear. Interest on your savings is ignored, which makes the estimate slightly conservative.
  • Total to save = your deposit plus stamp duty. Other buying costs (conveyancing, inspections, moving) are on top of that.
  • Take-home pay is worked out from 2026-27 tax rates, the Medicare levy and the low income tax offset. Two incomes are taxed separately. It does not include HECS repayments, salary sacrifice or private health cover.
  • Borrowing power assumes rent stops when you buy, repayments are tested at the rate below over 30 years, and your other spending stays the same.
  • Stamp duty lines and scheme price caps use each state's first home buyer settings as at August 2026. State budgets change these.

Every state, at a glance

Stamp duty is what first home buyers pay (or don't). The 5% deposit scheme is the government First Home Guarantee: buy with 5% deposit, no LMI, no income caps. Price caps below are as at August 2026 and change with government budgets.

VIC

Stamp duty: $0 up to $600,000 (new or established), discount from $600,001 to $750,000.

5% deposit cap: $950,000 Melbourne + Geelong, $650,000 rest of state.

NSW

Stamp duty: $0 up to $800,000, reduced rate to $1,000,000.

5% deposit cap: $1,500,000 Sydney + regional centres, $800,000 rest of state.

QLD

Stamp duty: new builds $0 at any price. Established: $0 up to $700,000, discount to $800,000.

5% deposit cap: $1,000,000 Brisbane, Gold Coast + Sunshine Coast, $700,000 rest of state.

WA

Stamp duty: $0 up to $600,000, reduced rate to $800,000.

5% deposit cap: $850,000 Perth, $600,000 rest of state.

SA

Stamp duty: new builds $0 with no price cap. Established homes get no first home discount.

5% deposit cap: $900,000 Adelaide, $500,000 rest of state.

TAS

Stamp duty: the first home exemption ended 30 June 2026, so duty currently applies.

5% deposit cap: $700,000 Hobart, $550,000 rest of state.

ACT

Stamp duty: $0 for eligible buyers from 1 July 2026, no price cap (conditions apply).

5% deposit cap: $1,000,000.

NT

Stamp duty: no general first home discount, but eligible new house and land packages can be duty free.

5% deposit cap: $750,000 Darwin, $600,000 rest of territory.
